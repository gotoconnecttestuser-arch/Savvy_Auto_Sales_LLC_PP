# SMS Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 17, 2026**

Savvy Auto Sales LLC (“Savvy Auto Sales,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This SMS Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information in connection with our customer-care text messaging program.

## Information we collect

When you interact with us or opt in to receive text messages, we may collect your name, mobile phone number, vehicle inquiry or purchase details, appointment information, and the content of messages you send us. We may also collect information you provide through our website, by phone, or at our dealership.

## How we use information

We use this information to respond to vehicle inquiries; schedule or update appointments; provide purchase, trade-in, financing, warranty, or service-related updates; send account-related notifications; and provide customer support.

Our SMS program is for customer-care messages related to your interactions with Savvy Auto Sales. We do not use this program to send promotional or marketing messages.

## SMS consent and information sharing

We do not sell or rent your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in information. **We do not share mobile information with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.** We may share information with service providers that help us operate our business or deliver messages, such as messaging and customer-support providers. Those providers may use the information only to perform services for us.

**Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that support delivery of our messaging program.**

Data Transfer Restrictions
Your data will not be transferred to external organizations, except where necessary to provide our services through essential service providers acting strictly on our behalf and not for their own use.

Protection Against Unauthorized Sharing
We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

## Your choices

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying **STOP**. For help, reply **HELP** or contact us using the information below. Opting out of texts will not prevent you from contacting us by phone, email, or in person.

## Data protection and retention

We use reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, provide requested services, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations.
